    • Nelson Mandela, former President of South Africa and global symbol of peace and freedom.

    Nelson Mandela was a revolutionary, political leader, and philanthropist who served as President of South Africa from 1994 to 1999. He was the country's first Black head of state and the first elected in a fully representative democratic election. His legacy lives on as a symbol of justice and reconciliation.
